Our Story

Founded with a passion for bringing authentic, rich Kenyan coffee traditions to your cup, Savanna Brew Café sources beans directly from the high-altitude volcanic soils of East Africa.

Every batch is roasted to perfection, capturing the bold flavors and warm community spirit of the savanna. Whether you're here to catch up with friends or find a quiet corner to work, we are dedicated to making every sip memorable.
